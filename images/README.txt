PHOTOS
- Car photos: upload them on the staff page (yourdomain.com/admin > Cars). They are saved in images/cars/
  automatically. You don't need to put them here yourself.
- Home page photo: put the file in THIS folder (example: images/shop.jpg), then open js/data.js and set
      heroImage: "images/shop.jpg",
  Use a landscape photo, about 1400 x 900 pixels, under 500 KB. Leave heroImage "" to show a car drawing.
- File names: lowercase, no spaces (use dashes).
